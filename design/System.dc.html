Grid

1440 desktop with 64px page gutters; 4-up product grid at 20px gap, 3-up inside a filtered listing, 2-up at 390 mobile with 12px gap.

Radius & edges

3px on every surface, 2px on badges, 50% on avatars and toggles only. Hairlines are 1px #DAD4C4; no drop shadows anywhere.

Spacing

Section rhythm 78px, card padding 20–26px, control gap 10–14px. All sibling groups use flex or grid with gap, never margins.

Touch targets

No tappable element under 44px on mobile. Desktop buttons are 56/46/38px; the quantity stepper stays 46px everywhere.
